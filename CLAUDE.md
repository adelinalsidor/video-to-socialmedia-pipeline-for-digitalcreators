# CLAUDE.md — Client Project (social media content automation)

> Context for any Claude Code session working in this folder. Read this before generating content or running a skill.

## Who the client is and who the content is for

**The client** — a makeup artist and content creator. Their target audience: **English-speaking women, 40+**. The client films one video per week (English, under 10 minutes).

**Important — what the client actually delivers:** the raw video the client films **is** the YouTube long-form post — not a brief that gets turned into a separate script. Only metadata (title, description, chapters, tags) is layered on top of it. Every other short format (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reel, TikTok) is a clip **cut from that same footage** (locally, with ffmpeg, via `video-repurposer`) — not a separate script. Only Substack, the Instagram Carousel, and Pinterest are text written from scratch based on the transcript. Full details per format: `strategy/repurposing-matrix.md`.

## Mandatory rule: language of generated content

**All content generated for publication (articles, captions, titles, descriptions, hashtags, subtitles) must be in ENGLISH.** Skill dialogue (questions via `AskUserQuestion`, terminal messages to the operator) can stay in the operator's language — that's for internal use only and never reaches the client or their audience.

## Where the client's voice comes from

**There is no single, all-purpose voice profile** for all content — a deliberate decision. For captions, hooks, and short clips (Instagram/TikTok/YouTube Shorts), the voice comes **directly from `video-sursa/{slug}/transcript.md`** for the current week — that's the client's actual speech, fresher and more accurate than any persistent profile could be.

The one exception: **the Substack article**, where spontaneous speech gets turned into written prose — there we use `tone/stil-scris-client.md` (produced by the `writing-style-client` skill), a short writing-style guide (not a personality/psychology profile), used **exclusively** by `substack-article-writer`.

If `tone/stil-scris-client.md` doesn't exist yet, don't invent a style — flag that it needs to be built first (run `writing-style-client`).

## The 5 platforms

Substack, YouTube (long-form + Shorts), Instagram (carousel + reel), TikTok, Pinterest. **Not** LinkedIn, X/Twitter, or Podcast — those were dropped from scope.

## What the pipeline intentionally does NOT do

- **No synthetic video** (no HeyGen avatar, no cinematic Veo) — all video is real footage of the client.
- **Doesn't start from a written article as a brief** — the brief is the weekly video, transcribed once.
- **Doesn't cut video through a paid external service** (Opus Clip/Vizard) — cutting into short clips happens locally, with ffmpeg, via the `video-repurposer` skill.

## Mandatory approval before publishing

Every post has a `review_status` in `config.json` (`pending_review` → `approved`), written by `upload-drive`. Substack requires the client's explicit approval (they can add their own input before approving). Instagram/TikTok/YouTube/Pinterest have a Sunday-evening deadline, ahead of the Monday posting cycle.
